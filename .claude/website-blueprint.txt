DHIDROID LABS
Website and editorial implementation notes
Based on the supplied company blueprint, version 1.0, 23 November 2026
Internal reference for repository contributors

Design
Keep the blue sky and green meadow, off-white paper, deep ink, glass navigation,
Inter body type and the italic Instrument Serif wordmark. Reuse the Tailwind theme
and shared components. Keep figures meaningful at mobile widths and pair diagrams
with ordinary HTML links and text. Keep original, optimized images in public/assets.

Content
Edit the JSON files in content/. The route registry feeds navigation, page bodies,
sitemap and metadata. Add a unique title and description, a clear user problem,
useful examples and one relevant next action. Maintain working inbound links.
An editorial byline belongs to a real person or the publishing studio.
Describe an illustrative concept as a proposal. Use completed-project language
only when evidence and publication rights have been confirmed.

Publication decisions
[PROPOSED] Engineering ideas into useful products. Primary audiences are founders,
operations teams and existing product teams. Discovery starts with one core workflow.
[VERIFY] Registered name, incorporation, office, official domain, business email,
phone, grievance contact, public developer names and links.
[ONLY IF TRUE] Enable design or careers entries after confirming capacity or a real
opportunity. Add client names, metrics, testimonials or credentials only with proof.
[LEGAL REVIEW] Review the actual enquiry processor, transfer locations, retention,
rights contact, cookie settings, website terms and required company disclosures.
Add verified home/footer disclosures before enabling a public launch.
[INTERNAL ONLY] Keep commercial tests, lead scoring, pricing hypotheses, outreach,
sales targets and competitor research out of public content and structured data.

Business development
Start with a fit conversation, offer discovery for uncertain scope, then estimate
a focused build. Agree deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, milestones, IP,
acceptance and support in writing. Review qualified enquiries, discovery calls,
wins and delivery variance against real baselines. Use opt-in, relevant outreach.

Source map from the supplied brief
Google Search Central SEO Starter Guide, Organization markup, XML sitemaps and
Core Web Vitals. W3C WCAG 2.2. MCA OPC FAQ, Companies Act section 12, ICSI Rule 26,
and PIB DPDP notices for professional review. Government MSME and competitor
references inform positioning only. Never portray sources as customers or partners.

Maintenance
Run npm run check and npm run test:e2e after a fresh install. Review phone-width
content and menus, all route relationships, privacy controls and contact failure
and retry behavior. Use README.md for environment setup and release procedures.
